YOUR DOWNLOAD WORKER

Both engines. One space.

Real requests use the local authenticated worker. Demo jobs simulate progress and save no media. Engine readiness is checked from worker status; selecting an engine never starts Java.

The queue hold affects waiting jobs only. Active jobs can be cancelled; pause, byte-resume and same-engine retry are not offered. If the first engine can’t take a link, the other engine takes it automatically and the job card names the engine that did. After an interrupted job, submit its source again explicitly. History may not retain the original source after restart.

Themes and reduced motion are saved in this browser. Worker history and saved links use the runtime's storage. Reported paths are not file-existence checks. Universe artwork uses finite animations; Reduce motion makes page changes immediate.
