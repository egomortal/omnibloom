# Personal Notes

*Hey, this is just a personal note from me, the developer. It isn't documentation.*

## About testing

I only have **one PS5, on firmware 13.60**. That's the only firmware I can test on.

Asking me to test other firmware versions is asking too much. **I can't test things I don't have.** If Omni works on your firmware, great. If it doesn't, I may not be able to do anything about it.

## This is a hobby

Omni is a hobby project. I work on it in my free time because I enjoy it, and it isn't meant to take over my life. I'll get to things when I can, and some things I may never get to.

## Feature requests (VR and the rest)

I see the requests for other firmware support, VR and other big features. I appreciate the interest, but these take hardware, time and money that I don't have right now.
