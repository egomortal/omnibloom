# Be kind. We’re all doing our part.

We’re here to build something together. Treat people with kindness and respect, and give each other room to learn.

- No harassment, bullying or personal attacks.
- Disagree with ideas respectfully. Explain your point without putting someone down.
- Help when you can. Be patient with questions, mistakes and different levels of experience.

Maintainers may remove harmful comments or limit participation to keep this space welcoming.

If something needs private attention, email **egomortalist@gmail.com**. Don’t post private incident details in an issue or pull request.
